Co-op mission

Two players work as one team on a large repair mission. Each of you edits your own lane, you both see each other's work live, and the team result goes on the co-op leaderboard with both Player IDs. Every team gets the same mission each week, so scores can be compared fairly.

Owner preview: rooms, invitations and team scores are off on this account. Practice levels work normally and record nothing.
