Privacy notice

What The Lost Protocol keeps, who can see it, and how to remove it.

Last updated 16 November 2026.

Who runs this site

The Lost Protocol is a final-year Computer Studies project by Joumana Amir Eldeeb. It is not a company, there are no adverts, and there are no paid plans. Questions about this notice can be sent through the Feedback button on the home page.

What is saved when you create an account

Your email address, your display name and your account type. The password is handled by Supabase and stored as a secure hash, so nobody working on the project can read it.

A learner profile also keeps the learner's name, age group, starting difficulty and Player ID. As the learner plays, the game saves progress, stars, scores, badges, achievements and the code kept in the editor.

What other people can see

The leaderboards show a learner's display name, Player ID, age group, difficulty, score, stars and the number of chapters finished. Co-op results show both players' names and Player IDs, the score and how long the match took.

Those pages are public. Test accounts are left out. A parent or educator sees only the learners they created. The site owner can see the account list, feedback and bug reports. Nothing else is shown to other users.

Children

A child should not create an account alone. A parent, guardian or teacher creates the profile in the Parent and Educator area, using their own email address for the adult account. The child then signs in with a Player ID and the password set for them. That adult can reset the password or delete the profile at any time.

Playing without an account

Guest play needs no name, email or sign-in. Progress stays in that browser and is not saved to an account, so it does not appear on the leaderboards and cannot be opened on another device.

Saved in your browser

The game keeps a sign-in token, local progress, settings such as volume and mood, and copies of pages for offline play. These are stored by your browser, not in advertising cookies. This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies and runs no tracking scripts.

Feedback and bug reports

Feedback is sent to a private Google Sheet and, when you are signed in, a copy is saved in the site database. A bug report is saved with the description you write, the page it relates to, basic browser information and any screenshot you choose to attach. Both are readable only by the site owner.

Where it is kept

Accounts, progress and scores are stored in Supabase. The website and its files are hosted by Vercel. Both are service providers and may keep data on servers outside your country. Standard security logs, such as the time of a request and an IP address, may be kept by those providers for a short time.

How it is protected

The site uses HTTPS. Database rules stop one account from reading another account's records, scores are checked on the server, and passwords are hashed. No website can promise perfect security, but the project does not collect more than it needs.

How long it is kept

An account and its progress stay until they are deleted. Deleting an account removes the profile, progress, scores and Player ID. Backup copies may remain for a short time before they are overwritten.

Your choices

You can delete your account from the Account page; a parent or educator can delete a learner profile from the Parent and Educator area. To ask a question or have something corrected, use the Feedback button on the home page.

What this site does not do

No adverts. No selling or renting of personal data. No profiling for marketing. No third-party analytics or tracking pixels.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
