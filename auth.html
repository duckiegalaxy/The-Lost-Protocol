Before you create an account

The short version:

  • We keep the display name, email address and account type you enter.
  • Learner progress, stars and scores are saved to the account so they follow it to another device.
  • The display name and Player ID appear on the public leaderboards.
  • The password is stored as a secure hash and cannot be read by us.
  • You can delete the account and all its progress from the Account page.
