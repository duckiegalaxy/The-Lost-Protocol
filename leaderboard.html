How ranking works. Higher score first, then more stars, then the player who reached it first. Equal score and stars share a rank. Every age group gets its own layout: sticker cards for Grades 1 to 6, a podium for Grades 7 to 10, a terminal grid for Grades 11 to 12 and a quiet list for college and adult players. Active today shows players who finished something since midnight, still ordered by total score because the board keeps one row per player. Search matches the display name or the Player ID. Co-op matches are scored by the server; test accounts, test matches and bonus results never appear.
