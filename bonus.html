Bonus sections

Optional sections open after you finish the five story sections. The two workshops are practice spaces: nothing there changes a score, leaderboard, co-op result or ranking, and every activity can be repeated with four levels of hints. For grades 7 and up, the Mastery missions add four extra game chapters: their stars and points count, but they never block the ending.
